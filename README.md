# Subtle Image Studio

A single-file web page (`index.html`) for gently reworking a photo and saving the result as a JPEG.

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step, server or internet connection is needed, and images never leave your device.

- **Load:** upload, drag and drop, or paste an image.
- **Light:** exposure, contrast, shadows, highlights, fade.
- **Colour:** temperature, tint, hue shift, saturation, vibrance.
- **Colour set:** remap to a limited palette taken from the image, or to warm, cool, teal & orange, vintage, pastel, sepia, black & white or duotone looks. Each has a strength slider, and there is a posterize slider.
- **Lines & texture:** soften or sharpen lines, smooth surfaces while keeping edges, dreamy glow, film grain.
- **Finish:** darkening or lightening vignette.
- **Tools:** hold-to-compare (or hold `C`), "Surprise me" random subtle edits, double-click a slider to reset it.
- **Export:** downloads a JPEG at full original resolution with adjustable quality.

# Ratio · A level Law (`ratio-law/`)

A single-file study app for the whole Eduqas GCE A level Law specification (A150QS). It covers Component 1 (law making, the legal system and the nature of law) and all four optional areas: contract, tort, criminal law and human rights. It also includes an exam-skills unit.

- **67 topics**, each with Learn, Cases, Explore (interactive tools), Flashcards, Quiz and Exam tabs.
- **600+ cases** in a searchable casebook; case names in the text open a summary card.
- **Statute book and glossary** of key sections and Latin terms.
- **Mock papers** for Components 1, 2 and 3. Components 2 and 3 are built from your three chosen areas.
- **Arcade games**, XP, streaks, badges and a mastery "bookshelf".

Open `ratio-law/index.html` in a browser. Source lives in `ratio-law/src/`. To validate the content and rebuild `index.html` (plus `dist/artifact.html`), run `node build.js` inside `ratio-law/`.
