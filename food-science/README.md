# Proof — WJEC Level 3 Food Science and Nutrition

**Proof** is a self-contained study app for the WJEC Level 3 Alternative Academic Qualification in Food Science and Nutrition (Extended Certificate, 4523QX). It covers Units 1–3 plus the optional Units 4 and 5.

## Use it

Download **`wjec-food-science-proof.html`** from the repository root and open it in any modern browser. It is a single file: no install, no server, no account. Progress is saved in that browser (and can be backed up from the Progress page).

## What's inside

- **52 topics.** They follow the specification's order across Units 1–5, plus exam technique and food maths. Each topic has:
  - **Learn:** notes with diagrams, worked examples and common mistakes, plus a red/amber/green specification checklist.
  - **Explore:** one or more of 57 interactive explorations. Examples include a sugar builder, protein complementation, a digestion journey, the Eatwell plate builder, glycaemic response, a front-of-pack label maker, the Stephan curve, a temperature probe, a fridge organiser, binary fission, growth conditions, spot-the-hazard, a HACCP plan builder, gelatinisation, sugar stages, egg cooking, meringue whisking, emulsions, gels, yeast fermentation, enzymic browning, a fair-test designer, a statistics calculator with t-test, regression, sampling bias, the CRAAP test and reflection frames.
  - **Flashcards** with spaced repetition (637 in total).
  - **Quiz:** 513 questions with explanations.
  - **Calculate:** 33 generators for unlimited food-maths practice (energy, salt, %RI, GL, BMI, BMR × PAL, costing, GP%, scaling, ratios, binary fission, risk ratings, statistics).
  - **Exam questions:** 141 exam-style and NEA-style questions with mark schemes.
- **Key facts:** critical temperatures, energy values, dietary recommendations, reference intakes, traffic-light thresholds, the 14 allergens, chopping-board colours, food-science temperatures and research statistics.
- **Assessment:** the unit formats, AO weightings, command words, NEA guides for Units 2, 4 and 5, and a UMS grade calculator.
- **Mock exam**, in two formats:
  - quick-fire, auto-marked papers;
  - full 80-mark Unit 1 and Unit 3 practice papers (Sections A, B and C), each with one of six case studies. You self-mark these against the mark schemes.
- **Arcade:** Deficiency Detective, Eatwell Sort, Pathogen Profiles, Temperature Check, True-or-False Blitz and a daily challenge.
- **Optional-unit setting:** choose Unit 4 or Unit 5 and the other one is hidden.
- **General features:** XP, levels, streaks and badges; light and dark themes; search (`/` or `Ctrl+K`); works on phones.

## Build

The source is in `src/`. Run `python3 build.py` to bundle everything into `index.html` and the root copy `../wjec-food-science-proof.html`.

## Accuracy

The content was written for this app from the WJEC specification and WJEC's learner notes. It is not official WJEC material. Where the learner notes contained errors, the app gives the correct science. See `SPEC-AUDIT.md` for coverage and the corrections made. Check the WJEC Assessment Guide and sample assessment materials, and follow your teacher's guidance.
