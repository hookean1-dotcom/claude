# Specification audit

Checked against **WJEC GCE AS/A Level Physics specification, Version 3 (October 2023)**, all 80 pages: the subject content for Units 1–4, the four Unit 4 options, Unit 5, the list of specified practical work and the appendices (practical techniques and mathematical requirements).

Every topic's **Specification checklist** in the app now reproduces the specification statements with their letters, so students can rate themselves against the actual wording.

## Removed (beyond the specification)

- Impulse and the area under a force–time graph (1.3)
- Launch angle for maximum range as a Unit 1 fact (kept only in Option C, where projectiles in sport are specified)
- Numerical relative strengths and ranges of the four interactions (1.7) — replaced by the specification's table
- Semiconductor vs metal charge-carrier densities (2.1, and the drift simulation's semiconductor mode)
- Phase difference in radians, 2πx/λ (2.4) — the specification requires only in phase and antiphase
- Harmonics and f = c/2L on strings (2.5)
- Escape velocity (4.2)
- The Hall voltage formula V_H = BI/nqt and why probes use semiconductors (4.4)
- Chain reactions and fusion ignition conditions in Unit 3 (fusion difficulties remain in Option D, where they are specified)
- Reactor moderators and control rods (Option D) — replaced by enrichment and breeding, which the specification names
- The ultrasound reflection-coefficient formula and half-value thickness (Option B) — the specification requires only I = I₀exp(−μx) and the significance of acoustic impedance
- Rotational equations of motion beyond α = (ω₂ − ω₁)/t (Option C)
- The term "ultimate tensile stress" (1.5)

## Practicals

All 26 specified practicals now appear with the specification's exact titles (6 in Unit 1, 10 in Unit 2, 6 in Unit 3, 4 in Unit 4). Unit 3 now has *Measurement of g with a pendulum* in place of a mass–spring practical, which is not on the list.
